## Provenance

Every icon in this library was generated from scratch with text-to-image models
using the prompt template documented in `docs/STYLE-GUIDE.md`, then cut out and
normalised by `tools/extract_alpha.py`. No asset was taken from a commercial
game, no asset was traced from existing artwork, and no asset contains
copyrighted characters or trademarks. That keeps the library usable under the
Foundry VTT EULA.
